About DJ's House of Cards & Comics 

A collector-built shop with a genuine hobby perspective.

DJ's House of Cards & Comics grew out of a personal collection and a real enthusiasm for sports cards, comics, and memorabilia. The goal is to make the site feel knowledgeable, welcoming, and enjoyable for collectors who like to browse, buy, sell, or trade.

Browse Sports Cards Talk With DJ

What the collection is built around

Many of the cards and comics featured on the original site came directly from DJ's personal collection. A longtime priority has been building a strong run of Hank Aaron and Willie Mays PSA-graded cards while continuing to add modern autograph and relic cards from baseball, basketball, and football.

The comics side carries that same enthusiasm, with an emphasis on Bronze Age books, recognizable keys, and collector favorites--from Star Wars #1 and Iron Man #100 to Batman #259, Detective Comics #475 and #476, Giant-Size Doc Savage #1, and many more across a wide range of price points.

  • A personal collection remains at the center of the catalog.
  • The card focus balances vintage stars, PSA goals, and modern autos and relics across three sports.
  • DJ's Benchtime Blog brings in collecting stories, hobby commentary, and the world of sports.
  • Photo galleries, direct contact, and DJ's Guarantee help keep the experience personal and trustworthy.

Built for collectors who enjoy the chase

The aim is not to feel like a generic storefront. It is to offer a more personal place to discover cards, comics, and collectibles, backed by enthusiasm for the hobby, clear communication, and a willingness to help fellow collectors find the right piece.
